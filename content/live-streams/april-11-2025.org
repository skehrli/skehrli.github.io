* Plain-Text Accounting in Emacs

- https://ledger-cli.org/

- https://plaintextaccounting.org

From the manual:

- https://ledger-cli.org/doc/ledger3.html#Emacs-org-Mode

#+begin_src emacs-lisp

  (use-package ledger
    :ensure nil
    :config
    (org-babel-do-load-languages
     'org-babel-load-languages
     '((ledger . t))))

#+end_src
